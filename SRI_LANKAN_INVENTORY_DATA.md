## 🚀 How to Initialize Data

### In Supplier Management
1. Navigate to **Inventory > Supplier Management**
2. Click the **🇱🇰 Add Sri Lankan Data** button
3. Wait for confirmation message
4. Data will be automatically populated

### In Purchase Order Management
1. Navigate to **Inventory > Purchase Order Management**
2. Click the **🇱🇰 Add Sri Lankan Data** button
3. Wait for confirmation message
4. Data will be automatically populated
